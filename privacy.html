Privacy / 隐私

Updated: 14 October 2026

Dad’s Night Light works offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics or tracking SDKs.

The app stores only your selected duration, brightness, language and introduction acknowledgement on your device. Clear app data or uninstall to remove these settings. Operating-system backups, if enabled, are governed by your device settings; the developer does not receive your preferences.

Screen brightness and the idle timer are adjusted temporarily to operate the night light. No camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos are accessed.

简体中文

爸爸的晚安灯离线运行，不收集、传输、出售或分享个人数据，不包含账号、广告、统计或追踪 SDK。

应用仅在手机本地保存时长、初始亮度、语言及使用说明确认状态。清除应用数据或卸载可删除这些设置。如启用系统备份，其行为由设备设置管理；开发者不会收到你的设置。

应用临时调整屏幕亮度和防休眠状态，不访问相机、麦克风、通讯录、位置或照片。

This website / 本网站

This public support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it may produce normal hosting security/access logs under the hosting provider's privacy statement. These are separate from the offline app; the developer adds no analytics, cookies or tracking scripts. If you email support, your email address and message are used to reply and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal data.
