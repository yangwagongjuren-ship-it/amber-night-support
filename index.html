Amber Night · 渐夜

When the dark feels too sudden, leave a little light beside you.
孩子怕黑时，留一盏陪伴灯，让光慢慢暗下来。

Why Amber Night? / 为什么做渐夜？

Amber Night began with a simple bedtime dilemma: a child is afraid when the light goes out, but a bright room keeps the conversation going. Choose a duration, leave a warm amber screen beside you, and let it fade gently into darkness. It is a companion light, not a promise of faster sleep.

孩子说不要关灯，可一直亮着又难以安静。渐夜的初心，就是陪孩子入睡时，留一点安心的光，再慢慢地暗下来，让自己和孩子有时间适应黑暗。

How to use / 使用方法

Choose a duration and brightness, then Begin. Hold the light for three seconds to reveal controls. Choose a short custom duration to test it.

选择时长和亮度，点击开始。长按屏幕三秒可显示控制。可先自定义较短的时长试用。

Why is my screen still on? / 为什么没有息屏？

The app fades to black and releases its wake lock. Your system controls actual screen lock. Enable automatic locking and check Always-On Display. LCD backlights may remain faintly visible until the screen switches off.

应用到时变黑并释放防休眠，实际息屏由系统控制。请开启自动锁定并检查息屏显示。LCD 在屏幕真正关闭前可能仍有背光。

What if I switch apps? / 切换应用会怎样？

Switching apps or locking restores your previous brightness while time continues to pass. Return before the deadline to continue the fade, or afterwards to see that it has finished. Tap a finished black screen to return to settings. Force-closing the app ends the session.

切换应用或锁屏时恢复手机原亮度，计时继续；到时前回来继续渐暗，到时后回来显示已结束。结束后轻点黑屏即可返回设置。强制关闭应用会结束本次计时。
