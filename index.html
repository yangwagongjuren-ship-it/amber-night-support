The story / 这盏灯的故事

I made this light because of the evenings I spend beside my child at bedtime.

When I switched off the light, my child was afraid of the dark. But if I left a phone light or night light on, it stayed just as bright, and my child found it hard to settle down.

I wanted a little light that could fade slowly, giving my child time to get used to the dark while I stayed beside them. That is how Dad’s Night Light began.

Choose a little warmth and a little time. Let the light grow dimmer while you share a quiet goodnight.

The light fades gently. Love stays.

Made by a dad, for anyone keeping someone company at bedtime. This light can accompany your presence; it cannot replace it.

做这盏灯，是因为每天晚上，我都会陪孩子睡觉。

关了灯，孩子害怕黑；可开着手机灯或小夜灯，灯光又一直那么亮，孩子迟迟静不下来。

我就想，能不能有一盏灯，先留一点让孩子安心的光，再慢慢、慢慢地暗下去？让孩子有时间适应黑暗，也让我在这段时间里，安安静静地陪着他。

于是，就有了“爸爸的晚安灯”。

选一点暖光，留一段时间。灯光一点点暗下去，我们一起，把晚安慢慢说完。

光慢慢暗，爱一直在。

它来自一位爸爸，也献给每一位用心陪伴的人。这盏灯陪着你们，却代替不了你在身边。

How to use / 使用方法

Choose a duration and brightness, then Begin. Hold the light for three seconds to reveal controls. Choose a short custom duration to test it.

选择时长和亮度，点击开始。长按屏幕三秒可显示控制。可先自定义较短的时长试用。

Why is my screen still on? / 为什么没有息屏？

The app fades to black and releases its wake lock. Your system controls actual screen lock. Enable automatic locking and check Always-On Display. LCD backlights may remain faintly visible until the screen switches off.

应用到时变黑并释放防休眠，实际息屏由系统控制。请开启自动锁定并检查息屏显示。LCD 在屏幕真正关闭前可能仍有背光。

What if I switch apps? / 切换应用会怎样？

Switching apps or locking restores your previous brightness while time continues to pass. Return before the deadline to continue the fade, or afterwards to see that it has finished. Tap a finished black screen to return to settings. Force-closing the app ends the session.

切换应用或锁屏时恢复手机原亮度，计时继续；到时前回来继续渐暗，到时后回来显示已结束。结束后轻点黑屏即可返回设置。强制关闭应用会结束本次计时。
